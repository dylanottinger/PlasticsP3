Services

The right partners and the right guidance, start to finish.

Pick one service or hand us the whole project. Our primary focus is finding the supplier partners best suited to your needs, then managing the project through to market.

Core service

Sales Representation & Supplier Matching

Every project needs a different kind of partner. We match you with molders and mold builders that have the facilities, capacity and quality systems for your specific part:

  • Large parts: high-tonnage molders and large-mold builders with heavy-duty cranes and the warehouse space to match.
  • Regulated products: medical, dental and pharmaceutical work with clean rooms and stringent quality assurance.
  • Turnkey supply: molders with robust assembly operations who can mold, assemble, warehouse and distribute from one facility.
