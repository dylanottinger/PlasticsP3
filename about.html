Our story

Experience you can build on

P3 Plastics is led by Andrew Vartanian, who brings 35 years of experience in every aspect of the plastics industry: product development, design and engineering, mold construction, sourcing, cost estimating, assembly, warehousing and distribution. Longer bio optional

That experience taught one lesson above all: when the right partnership of customer, service providers and suppliers is established from the outset, projects run smoothly and everyone succeeds. Finding and managing that partnership is what P3 Plastics does.
